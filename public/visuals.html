            Desk faders
          

          

          The desk's RGB sets the colour (hue), Colour the second colour or colour spread, Speed the speed, and Control 1-3 the sliders picked here.
          The preview runs in this browser. Send to wall puts the visual on the wall; the sliders move it while it's there.
        


        

          Virtual desk
          Plays the dot2 fixtures from here, to try them without the desk. Brightness sets the real LED wall brightness, and GO or BACK takes the wall back to the cues. Close this to hand back to the desk.
